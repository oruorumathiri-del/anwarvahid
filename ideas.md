# Design Direction — Ground-Truth Reconstruction

## Reference Commitment

The supplied **Anwar Vahid — Career Engineered** HTML file is the ground-truth specification. The rebuilt page will retain its editorial technical-career narrative, near-black and warm-paper contrast, acid-lime wayfinding, and immersive résumé structure, while resolving markup weight, asset delivery, responsive behavior, and image-quality defects.

## Chosen Design Philosophy: Editorial Industrial Portfolio

### Design Movement

Contemporary industrial editorial design: a precise blend of technical documentation, premium engineering catalogues, and long-form narrative portfolios.

### Core Principles

1. Use a strong dark/light sectional rhythm to create clear reading chapters without visual clutter.
2. Treat the lime accent as an instrument-panel signal, reserved for hierarchy, outcomes, and interaction states.
3. Present career evidence as structured operational information rather than generic résumé cards.
4. Use generous negative space, disciplined rules, and off-centre media to give the document an authored, credible character.

### Color Philosophy

Near-black pine (`#07110f`) conveys technical depth and authority, while warm paper (`#f5f2e9`) supports extended reading. Signal lime (`#b8e52d`) acts as a precise, high-visibility marker for key outcomes rather than decorative colour.

### Layout Paradigm

The page is a vertical field guide: a compact sticky command bar, an asymmetric opening statement with a media column, then full-width evidence chapters. Content uses ruled editorial systems and deliberately varied column ratios instead of a repetitive card grid.

### Signature Elements

The persistent AV monogram, numbered career milestones, and fine technical-rule dividers appear throughout the page. Image captions read as evidence labels, not lifestyle marketing.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interaction is direct and purposeful: anchor navigation, expanded role evidence, and restrained hover states. Controls should clarify document navigation and never compete with the career story.

### Animation

Use subtle opacity and translate entrances only where supported by user motion preferences. Hover and focus transitions must be fast, tactile, and limited to colour, border, and transform; no parallax or auto-playing visual distraction.

### Typography System

Use **Space Grotesk** for display statements and navigation, paired with **Source Sans 3** for long-form reading. Display lines are compact with assertive tracking; body copy remains calm, legible, and measured.

### Brand Essence

**Anwar Vahid turns technical detail into commercially accountable delivery for complex industrial teams.** Personality: precise, pragmatic, composed.

### Brand Voice

Headlines state a career position clearly; CTAs invite a practical next step. Example lines: “Technical detail, carried through to delivery.” and “Trace the roles, decisions and outcomes.” Generic filler is excluded.

### Wordmark & Logo

Use a high-contrast AV engineering monogram inside a squared technical frame, paired with a tightly tracked “ANWAR VAHID” wordmark and a smaller “CAREER ENGINEERED” descriptor.

### Signature Brand Color

**Signal Lime — `#b8e52d`**.

## Style Decisions

Signal Lime is reserved for navigation signals, metrics, section numbers, primary actions, and decisive operating terms; it is not a general decorative highlight. The AV mark must read as a compact squared engineering monogram paired with the tightly tracked “ANWAR VAHID / CAREER ENGINEERED” lockup. Image treatment must read as operational evidence through documentary industrial sites, drawings, fabrication, field assessment, and stakeholder/process scenes, with layout variation preventing a stock-gallery impression.
